Drills — 2 Players

Drill Tags Key Points Detail Link
Diagonal Corner Drill #Corner Different angles, different rules / Ball fast → turn / Early preparation before ball bounces / Play the ball high fast / Play the ball in front of you Play balls to the right or left foot and practice following the ball movement. otronivel
Bajada Drill #Bajada Early preparation → racket up + follow ball with non-active hand / Fast acceleration, no wrist Follow the bounce of the ball — control over power. hellopadel

Drills — 4 Players

Drill Tags Key Points Detail Link
Chiquita #Defense 2 players defending vs. 2 players at the net → defending players must force an easy ball → play chiquita → net game or lob that switches roles No lobs in defense position allowed. Chiquita cross only when teammate knows → point starts when playing the chiquita. Hello Padel
No Bounce Game #Overheads Attacking players can't let the ball bounce / Defending team should make the attackers move No bounce for attacking team. Otro
Lob #Lob 2 players defending vs. 2 players at the net → defending players can only play a lob if the ball bounces off a wall Lobs only allowed after wall. Hello Padel
