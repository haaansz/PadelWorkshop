## Drills

| What | Tags | Key Points | Detail | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diagonal Corner Drill | #Corner | Different angles, different rules / Ball fast → turn / Early preparation before ball bounces / Play the ball high fast / **Play the ball in front of you** | Play balls to the right or left foot and practice following the ball movement | [otronivel](https://youtu.be/0BsTOKOujIs?list=PL_l3gJM1EscI-djf3oDkvoWCgbEQplTEz&t=171) |
| Bajada Drill | #Bajada | Early preparation → racket up + follow ball with non-active hand / Fast acceleration, **no wrist** | Follow the bounce of the ball — control over power | [hellopadel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1uaT2O_HS4M) |
| Defense | #Defense | Detect an easy ball → change direction + move forward → chiquita + move forward | | |
| Overheads Right Shoulder | #Overheads | High balls → cross to fence / High balls → vibora straight | Right-side players only | [hellopadel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lG1heZGqz-U) |
| Overheads Left Shoulder | #Overheads | High balls → gancho/bandeja to the fence | Right-side players only | [hellopadel](https://youtu.be/XXfAoP8CZ7g) |
